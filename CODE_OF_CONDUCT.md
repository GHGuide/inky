# Code of conduct

Inky is for everyone: people who've never touched a terminal, and people who write browser engines. We want both to feel welcome here.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback about the work rather than the person, credit others, and help newcomers find their way.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; make sexual, violent or discriminatory remarks; publish someone's private information; or keep pushing after someone asks you to stop.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn, suspend or ban people who don't follow it, in proportion to what happened.

If something happens to you or you see it, message a maintainer privately on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
